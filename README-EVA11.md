**This is the Chrysler Electronic Voice Alert (11-function).  
This uses the TMS5110 chip.  
ROM data obtained from <https://mdk.cab/game/eva11>  
Below are the phrases and their hex addresses.**

0000 - Don't forget your keys  
0116 - Headlights are on  
022A - Please fasten your seatbelts  
0386 - A door is ajar  
04A1 - Parking brake is on  
05B8 - Fuel  
0621 - Engine oil pressure  
0725 - Washer fluid  
07FA - Prompt service is required  
09AE - Engine is overheating  
0AE4 - Electrical system is malfunctioning  
0CC7 - All monitored systems are functioning  
0E5C - Thank you  
0ECD - Your  
0F05 - Is low  
0F88 - 1 second pause
